### Hey, we're Capacium

We build open infrastructure for packaging, discovering, verifying, and managing agent capabilities across modern AI ecosystems.

Most capability artifacts today live in fragmented repos, registries, prompts, MCP servers, workflows, and tool-specific formats. We believe the future is not in isolated assets, but in a shared capability layer: a structured foundation where skills, bundles, MCP servers, templates, and workflows can be installed, discovered, verified, and reused across environments.

---

### What we do

We design and develop open capability infrastructure for AI agents and hybrid human-agent systems.

Capacium provides the foundation for:
- capability packaging
- discovery and exchange
- trust and verification
- registry and indexing
- interoperability across tools, runtimes, and ecosystems

Our ecosystem includes the Capacium CLI as the core, Exchange as the discovery layer, Crawler as the ingestion layer, and supporting bridges and distribution surfaces around them.

---

### Our Vision

> To establish an open, trusted capability layer for the agent ecosystem.

We envision a future where agent capabilities are as structured, portable, and trustworthy as software packages — and where humans, agents, tools, and systems can build on shared standards instead of fragmented silos.

### Our Mission

> We make agent capabilities installable, discoverable, and trustworthy through open standards, clear packaging models, verifiable provenance, and modular infrastructure.

### Core Values

Trust through verification. Clarity through structure. Openness through standards. Composability by design. Practical usefulness over hype.
